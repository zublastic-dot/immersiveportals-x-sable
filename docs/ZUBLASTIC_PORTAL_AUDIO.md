# Portal sound transport

The positional source belongs to the world that produced it. The listener belongs to the
physical player world, independently of the portal currently being rendered. Sound instances,
native channels and stream positions retain their original identity. IP does not replay a
jukebox when the listener crosses a portal.

## Current boundary

This first milestone covers ordinary positional sounds and native jukebox playback. SPA's
managed propagated loops, sonic booms, managed long-range emitters and recognized native
propeller/thruster/train partners retain their native playback ownership. Their independently
scheduled OpenAL gain and position writes are not yet portal-aware; this release does not
claim portal transport for those voices. Classification uses SPA's own marker interfaces,
resolved emitter metadata and exact sound policies, once per retained sound identity. Sable
delegates are unwrapped for ownership only. No extra live-world query runs on the audio thread.

Transport selects the shortest direct or single rectangular, unit-scale portal route among
loaded endpoint worlds, bounded to 128 nearby portals and a 256-block path. A route passes
through the aperture. OpenAL receives its bearing and full source-to-entry plus exit-to-listener
distance, so linear attenuation runs once. Remote sources without a route retain a silent
channel and recover if the path becomes available; world unload stops them. Native stop and
reload operations remain authoritative. Relative/UI sounds and music remain native.

Source scopes cover ClientLevel playback, redirected packets and LevelRenderer jukeboxes.
Direct SoundManager callers without an explicit producing world fall back to the actual player
world. This cannot infer a remote dimension from an arbitrary third-party SoundInstance.
Remote biome ambience must first be generated by its owning mod; transport does not manufacture
Dynamic Surroundings birdsong in an otherwise unticked remote biome.

## Sound Physics Aeronautics

The optional adapter targets the inspected Aeronautics acoustic-scene API. It leaves SPA's
active Sable provider and Create overlay installed for ordinary sounds. Routed voices receive
a composite immutable scene: source geometry is transformed into the listener frame,
with rays crossing only at the real aperture. Local material coordinates remain attached to
their owning root/Sable space. SPA still evaluates its own absorption and reflections and
applies its existing category configuration. Records are not an occlusion test when the
owner's SPA record-processing setting is disabled.

Copies run on the client thread with a shared cooperative four-millisecond budget per 50ms,
at most four cached requests, 32768 copied cells and sixteen spaces per request. Publication
is immutable; the audio thread never reads a remote live Level or changes Minecraft.level.
Copies refresh at 250ms and expire rather than becoming permanent stale geometry. Unknown
cells and crossings outside the aperture are not verified clear paths. A pending capture skips
SPA's wrong-world evaluation; the native routed voice continues, and bounded refresh applies
the available scene later. Initial acoustic convergence remains a limitation.

Create contraption or straddling geometry inside a requested region is explicitly unavailable
until its clipped immutable representation is integrated. Scaled/nonrectangular portals and
multiple-portal paths are outside this first transport implementation. Directional diffraction,
physical wave travel delay and portal Doppler are not newly simulated here.

## Verification

`imm_ptl_client_debug portal_sound` records bounded native-source IDs, ownership, path, panning
position and SPA capture/evaluation state. Compare these with SPA's flight recorder and actual
output audio; a route log alone does not prove audible output or correct filtering.

Use an actual source-world note-block event for acoustic tests, and a jukebox for continuity:
open aperture, source-side blocker, listener-side blocker, look away, cross both directions,
stop source, remove link and unload/reload. Restore all fixture blocks and player state.
Direct `/playsound` to a player is not proof of source-world provenance. Create train movement,
belts and other physical transport are separate work from this audio path.
